## 1. Scope and privacy method

The analysis used only fields needed for professional opportunity planning: general professional profile, positions, skills, education, projects, volunteering, aggregated connections, invitation direction and dates, message subjects, events, searches, learning activity and Marketplace metadata. It excluded private identity, LinkedIn inferences, e-mail addresses, phone numbers, street address, birth date, login data, security challenges, ad-targeting data and private job-application answers.

Names, personal profile URLs and message bodies are not included in this report. Connections are analyzed by role and sector signals, not by gender, age, orientation, nationality, appearance or presumed personal intent. A recommendation must be based on an explicit, current and service-relevant signal.

### Probability and scoring rule

The initial prioritization uses a score from 0 to 100:

```text
priority = 0.35 × explicit intent
         + 0.30 × portfolio fit
         + 0.20 × signal recency
         + 0.15 × observed reciprocity
```

| Score | Recommended use |
|---:|---|
| 70–100 | High priority: personalized contact and a concrete offer |
| 50–69 | Medium priority: nurture, share evidence and validate a need |
| 0–49 | Exploratory: opt-in activity, content or more research |

True probabilities require labeled outcomes. For each goal, a simple Beta model can start with a uniform prior:

```text
posterior probability = (positive outcomes + 1) / (observable attempts + 2)
```

Define a positive outcome before outreach: a qualified discovery call or signed pilot for sales; an accepted co-event or referral agreement for partnerships; reciprocal conversation and opt-in participation for friendship; a confirmed itinerary for tourism; or recurring contribution for volunteering.

## 6. Sales strategy

### Priority segments

1. **Technology recruiting and HR Tech.** Offer a technical architecture or delivery service for their clients’ modernization, private AI, Java/.NET, ERP or commerce needs. The desired outcome is a referral to a CTO or buyer.
2. **Founders, commercial partners and executives.** Offer a four-to-six-week pilot containing diagnosis, architecture, a minimal integration, a demo and a continuation decision.
3. **Finance, insurance and credit.** Present Open Banking, payments, explainable risk, auditability and private data. Separate technical prototypes from regulated financial promises.
4. **B2B commerce and supply chain.** Use JFXAI4OHS and JFXETL4DE to show catalogues, procurement, ERP, analytics and operational automation.
5. **Organizations with legacy systems.** Use JFXLEGACY2MODERN for a modernization assessment that ends with a prioritized route and proof of concept.

### Thirty-day sequence

- Select 20 contacts with a documented professional reason for fit; store only the fields needed for follow-up.
- Publish three evidence pieces: an architecture diagram, a local RAG/MCP demo and a transactional integration case.
- Send a short invitation tied to one problem and one relevant repository.
- After a reply, propose a 20-minute discovery call covering current problem, data/integration constraints and a measurable pilot result.
- Send a one-page proposal with scope, deliverables, dependencies, price or range and the next decision.
- Track `identified → contacted → replied → discovery → proposal → pilot → won/lost`, with a next-step date and follow-up permission.

### Consultative message

> I build open architectures that integrate AI, RAG/MCP and microservices with Java/.NET, ERP, payments and e-commerce systems. Your work in [sector or function] made me think there may be a useful overlap. Do you have a modernization, integration or internal-knowledge problem worth exploring in a short call? If there is a fit, I can outline a bounded pilot with deliverables and success criteria.

Do not claim savings, performance or return without a comparable measurement. The export records activity, not contracts, prices or revenue.

## 13. Limitations and governance

- The 66 connections do not represent the entire LinkedIn network.
- Invitation records do not provide a complete reliable acceptance state, so connection rate cannot be calculated.
- Marketplace records contain no monetary amounts or closed-won status, so revenue and margin cannot be estimated.
- Search and follow events describe account activity, not a third party’s buying intent.
- The five events marked `RELINQUISHED` do not prove attendance.
- Profiles can be stale, and a title does not prove purchasing authority.
- LinkedIn activity must not be scraped, bulk-messaged or exported to another system without applicable permission and compliance with platform terms.
- Personal and contact data should remain in the authorized system with limited retention and access controls.

Before automating any recommendation, approve the positive-outcome definitions, contact policy, allowed segments, retention, exclusions, consent record, message review and pipeline owner. Review the report again for the second half of September using the same definitions so the scores can be calibrated against real outcomes.
