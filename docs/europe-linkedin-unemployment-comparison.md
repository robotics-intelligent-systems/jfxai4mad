## 2. Cited labor-market context

The original text identifies the Czech Republic, Poland and Malta as examples of lower unemployment and Finland and Spain as examples of higher unemployment. It also places the European Union average at around 6%.

## 3. Methodological corrections

For a valid comparison:

1. Use the same observation period for all countries.
2. Cite the official unemployment definition and whether the figure is seasonally adjusted.
3. Clearly distinguish LinkedIn **registered members** from **active users**.
4. Do not assume higher unemployment means greater availability for a specific proposal.
5. Separate EU countries from European countries outside the EU when comparing labor policies.

## 4. Recommended sources

- **Eurostat** for unemployment in the European Union.
- National statistical offices where appropriate.
- Official data or documented LinkedIn estimates for audience size.
