# Online-Marriage Market Distribution Scenario

> **Editorial status:** exploratory allocation hypothesis migrated from an uncited Spanish planning note. The counts are a proposed LinkedIn search/outreach allocation, not validated market size, available audience, client demand, or a forecast. The source provides no dataset or method for its conversion-rate estimates.

## Purpose and scope

The source proposes distributing 30,000 LinkedIn-targeting opportunities across U.S. states and three international markets for online marriage and destination-wedding services, excluding China. It frames Utah as the lead market and suggests using international-marriage, cross-border, visa, and same-sex-marriage interest as targeting signals.

This note preserves the proposed allocation for planning review. It does not establish that the listed jurisdictions allow a particular couple to marry remotely, that a remotely solemnized marriage will be recognized where either person lives, or that the source's prospect counts and conversion rates are achievable. Courtly is one example of a provider in this category: its website describes an online marriage process that includes license coordination, a video ceremony with a U.S. officiant, and marriage-document handling. Courtly says it is not a law firm and that acceptance by foreign governments or immigration officials is outside its control; see its [FAQ](https://www.courtly.com/faqs). This provider example is contextual and does not validate the market allocations or conversion estimates.

## Conversion estimates and source inconsistencies

The source gives no definition of “conversion” (for example, reply, qualified inquiry, paid booking, or completed ceremony), observation period, sample size, denominator, attribution method, or confidence interval. Treat every rate above as an unsupported planning assumption; do not use it for revenue, hiring, or market-size forecasts.

The source's category-level ranges also conflict with its detailed rates. Its U.S. Tier 1 summary gives 4.2%, while California is listed at 4.5%; its Tier 2 range is 2.5%–3.2%, while Virginia is listed at 3.5% and Alabama at 2.2%. Reconcile the definitions and evidence before computing a weighted conversion rate.

A useful pilot would define each funnel stage separately, use a small and consent-respecting sample, record only necessary professional interaction data, and compare observed results with an explicit baseline. A response or connection is not a qualified lead, booking, marriage, or legal-recognition outcome.

## Platform and legal boundaries

LinkedIn's official guidance sets a maximum of 30,000 first-degree connections **per member** and says invitation limits and restrictions apply. That network ceiling does not mean a member can acquire 30,000 relevant prospects, and it does not establish a universal weekly invitation quota. LinkedIn recommends contacting people with a clear professional reason and warns that excessive or unwanted invitations can lead to restrictions. A 30,000-record research plan must distinguish search results, unique prospects, invitations, accepted connections, inquiries, and customers; it must not assume a pooled account limit or use automation to bypass platform controls. See [LinkedIn invitation restrictions](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a551012?lang=en) and [ways to connect professionally](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a541671).

Utah County currently documents a remote ceremony hosted by a deputy clerk over Zoom, with two adult witnesses, and warns that a remotely solemnized Utah marriage may be invalid in the country where the parties reside. Courtly describes itself as a private online-marriage service coordinating the license, video ceremony with a U.S. officiant, and document delivery; it explicitly says it is not a law firm and does not determine foreign recognition. This is a jurisdiction-specific example, not a general rule for all U.S. states or international markets. Check the issuing authority's current requirements and obtain qualified advice on recognition and immigration consequences for the people and countries involved before making service claims. See [Utah County remote ceremonies](https://clerk.utahcounty.gov/marriage-license/ceremonies) and its [remote ceremony scheduling information](https://clerk.utahcounty.gov/marriage-license/ceremonies/schedule-remote).

Do not infer relationship status, sexual orientation, immigration needs, willingness to marry, or legal eligibility from a person's nationality, location, occupation, profile, or network. Keep professional outreach transparent, relevant, opt-in where appropriate, and separate from personal compatibility or dating activity. No outreach or contact acquisition was performed in this migration.

## Validation gates

Before using this scenario operationally:

1. Define what the 30,000 units represent and whether they are search results, unique professional prospects, or another measurable inventory.
2. Verify the service, eligibility, identity, witness, timing, fee, certificate, recognition, and privacy requirements directly with each relevant authority and provider; date-stamp each check.
3. Remove unsupported market and conversion claims or replace them with sourced observations and a documented pilot method.
4. Review channel terms, privacy obligations, and outreach purpose; do not use sensitive personal attributes as targeting proxies.
5. Reconcile the destination count and category-level versus state-level conversion definitions before any forecast.
