The source's opening and conclusion assert that a marriage involving a child
under ten is necessarily unlawful and automatically void in Djibouti, including
with parental authorization. That categorical domestic-law conclusion is **not
established by this limited review**. Child marriage remains a child-protection
issue; documenting legal uncertainty does not endorse it.

## Selected statutory comparison

The official identifier is **Law No. 152/AN/02/4ème L**, rather than the draft's
152/PR/02. In the published 2002 text, Article 13 specifies eighteen; Article 14
also provides a minor-marriage exception. Article 7 addresses spouses' and
guardian consent, while Article 8 lists record particulars. The reviewed
provisions do not express the draft's specific under-ten incapacity rule or
under-thirteen criminal threshold. [Official Family Code](https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/loi-n152-an-02-4eme-l-portant-code-de-la-famille/)

This is a comparison with a historical enactment, not a verified consolidation
of current law. It does not resolve applicability to foreign nationals, later
amendments, judicial practice or interaction with other codes.

## International child-protection framework

Article 21(2) of the African Charter on the Rights and Welfare of the Child calls
for prohibition of child marriage and betrothal, legislation specifying eighteen
as the minimum marriage age, and compulsory registration. [African Union text](https://au.int/sites/default/files/documents/30913-doc-acrwc-en.pdf)

The African Committee's table records Djibouti's ratification on 3 January 2011
and deposit on 25 January 2011. [Ratification table](https://www.acerwc.africa/en/member-states/ratifications)

The UN Treaty Collection records Djibouti's CRC ratification on 6 December 1990.
Participation alone does not settle direct domestic enforceability, the effect of
declarations/reservations, or automatic nullity in a particular case.
[UN Treaty Collection](https://treaties.un.org/Pages/ViewDetails.aspx?chapter=4&clang=_en&mtdsg_no=IV-11&src=UNTSONLINE)
