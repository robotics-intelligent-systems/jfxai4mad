# Rio Carnival calendar: 2027 schedule and 2028–2030 projections

Source: `docs/El Carnaval de Río no.txt`, migration entry 60. Editorial review: 10 October 2026.

## Calendar basis

The Spanish draft explains that Carnival moves each year with Christian Easter. Ash Wednesday is 46 days before Easter Sunday. It describes a typical February–early March season, but that range is approximate. Ash Wednesday marks the start of Lent; it does not end every Carnival event, since the Champions' Parade and some street events can follow it.

## Future editions: calendar projections

The draft labels these as “official” Friday-to-Ash-Wednesday periods. Here they are retained as calculated calendar windows. Only the selected 2027 parade dates above were checked against organizer announcements; future parade programming is not confirmed by the date calculation.

| Year | Friday through Ash Wednesday | Following Saturday / draft Champions' Parade date | Easter Sunday used for calculation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2027 | 5–10 February | 13 February | 28 March |
| 2028 | 25 February–1 March | 4 March | 16 April |
| 2029 | 9–14 February | 17 February | 1 April |
| 2030 | 1–6 March | 9 March | 21 April |

Dates use the Gregorian Easter calculation: Ash Wednesday = Easter − 46 days; Friday = Ash Wednesday − 5 days; following Saturday = Ash Wednesday + 3 days. These offsets reproduce all four rows in the draft.

## Street Carnival and project use

The draft says street parties (*blocos*) formally start two to three weeks before the main window and continue until the following weekend. Retain this as an approximate source claim: individual routes, dates and permits require the municipal programme. The Sambódromo programme and street Carnival have separate schedules.

For JFXAI4MAD destination planning, store event category, local date, organizer, reference URL, verification date and confirmation status separately. Recheck published programmes before using projections for bookings or itineraries.
