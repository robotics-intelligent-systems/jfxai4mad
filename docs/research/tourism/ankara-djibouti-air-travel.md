# Ankara–Djibouti Air Travel: Routes, Costs and Entry Research

**Status:** translated travel-planning note. Airline combinations, timings and prices below are undated source claims, not confirmed schedules or current quotations. Selected entry and health checks are separated from the translation.

## 1. Air routes and principal airlines — source translation

To travel from Ankara (Esenboğa — **ESB**) to Djibouti (Ambouli — **JIB**), the source states that there are no direct commercial flights and that at least one or two connections through the Middle East or East Africa are required. This absence-of-service claim has not been independently verified.

The source describes these combinations as the most common and efficient operational options; availability and efficiency remain unverified:

| Airline / grouping named in source | Connection route | Source average duration | Source description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Turkish Airlines | Ankara (ESB) → Istanbul (IST) → Djibouti (JIB) | 8–11 hours | Most direct and integrated option with Türkiye's flag carrier. |
| Qatar Airways | Ankara (ESB) → Doha (DOH) → Djibouti (JIB) | 12–16 hours | Excellent onboard service and a connection at Hamad Airport. |
| EgyptAir | Ankara (ESB) → Cairo (CAI) → Djibouti (JIB) | 10–14 hours | Good regional option for the Red Sea and North Africa. |
| Flynas / AJet | Ankara (ESB) → Jeddah (JED) → Djibouti (JIB) | 13–18 hours | Combination of low-cost or regional Middle Eastern airlines. |

The descriptions retain the source's subjective assessments. They do not establish that each airline operates every leg, sells a through-ticket or belongs to a common alliance. No numerical flight frequency was supplied.

## 3. Key travel factors — source translation

- **Visa requirement:** the source states that citizens of most countries need to apply for an official Djibouti e-visa or arrange a visa before travel. It does not identify nationality-specific exemptions or eligibility.
- **Overnight connections:** for Istanbul (IST) or Doha (DOH), the source recommends at least **2–3 hours** between flights to avoid checked-baggage complications. This interval is a source planning suggestion, not a verified minimum connection time or transfer guarantee.
- **Health requirements:** the source states that an international yellow-fever vaccination certificate is indispensable for entering Djibouti. This blanket statement needs the qualification below.

## 4. Selected official-source checks

Checked on **2026-10-10**. The traveller's nationality, passport, transit itinerary and travel date remain unspecified.

### Visa scope

Djibouti's [official e-visa portal](https://www.evisa.gouv.dj/) is available for checking applications. Its existence does not verify the source's “most countries” generalization. The [UK government's entry guidance](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/djibouti/entry-requirements) describes visa requirements and online application for British-citizen passport holders; that nationality scope must not be extended to every traveller. Confirm the applicable visa and transit conditions with Djibouti's authorities and each transit country before booking.

### Yellow-fever certificate qualification

The [UK government's entry guidance](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/djibouti/entry-requirements) makes the certificate requirement conditional on arrival from a yellow-fever transmission-risk country. [NaTHNaC / TravelHealthPro](https://travelhealthpro.org.uk/countries/djibouti) specifies travellers aged **1 year or over** arriving from risk countries, including those transiting **more than 12 hours** through an airport in a risk country. The source's universal requirement is therefore not retained as operational guidance. The actual itinerary and relevant health advice determine applicability.

### Route and fare validation still required

Check every leg for the intended dates with the operating carrier, including airport, ticketing arrangement, baggage transfer and published minimum connection time. Obtain dated fare quotations before using the cost ranges. This migration does not verify the four route combinations or recommend a booking.
