Source: `docs/Para planificar una escapada.txt`, migration entry 62. Editorial review: 10 October 2026.

The Spanish draft proposes a six-day, five-night Rio de Janeiro holiday using an online travel agency such as Atrápalo Perú. The following translation retains its estimates and activities; no live package, dated airfare or hotel inventory was checked. Compare the [Lima–Rio bus research](lima-rio-de-janeiro-bus-travel.md) and [Rio Carnival calendar](rio-carnival-calendar.md) before choosing dates.

## Flight: translated source and qualifications

- **Route:** Lima–Rio de Janeiro–Lima, approximately five to six hours for a direct flight. The draft also mentions a short connection and names LATAM and GOL as examples. Airline names do not establish a current nonstop service; connecting elapsed time must include both legs and the layover.
- **Round-trip fare:** from USD 393, approximately S/1,358, described as an economy base fare on a travel platform. Preserve this as an undated source estimate, not a bookable offer or a verified Atrápalo price.
- **Baggage:** the draft says a personal item and cabin bag are generally included and a 23 kg checked bag costs extra. Actual allowance depends on fare family, airline, route and ticket conditions. LATAM's official baggage page distinguishes included allowances by fare; do not apply the draft's assumption to every base fare or to GOL.

For international arrival use the airport code in the ticket. Galeão (GIG) is the international airport. Santos Dumont (SDU) serves domestic flights and is a possible endpoint after a Brazilian domestic connection, not an equivalent nonstop Lima arrival. Include any airport change in the quoted transfer plan.

## Package budget: source figures and arithmetic

The draft labels this table “per person”, but the hotel occupancy basis is missing.

| Component | Draft USD | Draft S/ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Round-trip flight | 393 | 1,358 |
| Five-night midrange South Zone hotel | 300–450 | 1,100–1,650 |
| Return airport–hotel transfers | 40–60 | 150–220 |
| Source total | 733–903 | 2,608–3,228 |

Both column totals correctly add their respective component ranges. Their currency conversions use inconsistent implied exchange rates, so retain the columns as separate inherited estimates; no current exchange rate is certified. The five-night USD hotel figure equals five times USD 60–90, but that calculation alone does not establish a per-person cost.

A reviewable quote should define `per-person total = return flight + five-night room total / occupants + shared transfers / passengers + individual extras`. Use actual billing units; a transfer may be quoted per vehicle or per passenger. Record dates, currency, exchange-rate date if converting, taxes, fees, baggage, meals, attraction tickets, local transport, insurance and cancellation terms. The draft totals omit or do not specify these extras. Six days/five nights describes the destination stay; flight schedules can change the full door-to-door trip duration.

The source notes that dynamic packages vary by season, booking lead time, Carnival and New Year. Do not use these ranges as a confirmed Carnival package.
