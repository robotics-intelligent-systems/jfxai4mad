# Tucumán Carnival and Medellín cultural festivals

This note processes the additional Spanish draft about Argentina and Colombia.
It compares regional cultural tourism, not two equivalent Carnival programmes.
Selected checks were performed on 11 October 2026; undated source descriptions
remain distinct from confirmed annual schedules.

## Tucumán, Argentina

### Fiesta Nacional de la Pachamama, Amaicha del Valle

The draft places this celebration in the Calchaquí valleys during Carnival
season and describes a combination of Indigenous spirituality, community
celebration and Carnival play. It names four figures: Pachamama (an elder woman
representing wisdom), Ñusta (young earth), Yastay (protector of animals) and
Pujllay (a Carnival spirit). It describes blessings, copla singing and games
with flour, coloured paint, foam and water.

The official [Tucumán destination page](https://www.tucumanturismo.gob.ar/articulos/articulo/722/amaicha-del-valle)
identifies February as a visiting period associated with this festival.
The [festival account](https://www.tucumanturismo.gob.ar/articulos/articulo/485/amaicha-del-valle-y-su-fiesta-de-la-pachamama-una-celebracion-de-tradicion-y-gratitud)
supports its ritual and cultural character. Exact annual dates, character
selection rules and every game listed above were not independently established
in this review. Do not confuse the Carnival-season festival with other
Pachamama observances elsewhere in the year.

### Popular parades: San Miguel de Tucumán and El Cadillal

The source describes provincial/municipal “Carnaval de la Familia” events:
murgas, local comparsas, land or nautical floats and free outdoor concerts
featuring cumbia, folklore and cuarteto. These are retained as source descriptions.
No specific edition, organiser announcement, nautical parade or free-entry
condition was verified. Treat each locality and edition as a separate event.

Argentina's official [2026 holiday calendar](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/feriados)
provides the Carnival holiday context. A public holiday does not confirm the
dates of an individual festival or its programme.

## Medellín, Colombia

### Feria de las Flores

The source describes Medellín's major cultural festival: silleteros from Santa
Elena carrying floral arrangements, classic cars, flower plazas, trova and
neighbourhood music stages. It uses “equivalent to a carnival” as a comparison
of festive atmosphere. For project classification, retain the event's proper
name and category: a flower/cultural festival with its own annual calendar.

The [municipal 2026 page](https://www.medellin.gov.co/es/feria-de-flores)
confirms 31 July–9 August 2026 and lists silletero, classic-car, flower-plaza and
trova programming. These are past-edition dates, not a forecast for 2027.
“August” is a useful seasonal shorthand but can omit the July opening.

### February activity and excursions

The draft proposes Barranquilla-inspired comparsas in Medellín cultural venues
and tourism to Comuna 13, Metrocable, Pueblito Paisa, Guatapé and El Peñol.
These remain itinerary candidates; no February performance, timetable or
transport arrangement was verified. Guatapé and El Peñol are separate destinations,
not Medellín neighbourhoods.

The draft's phrase “national holidays in February” should not be transferred
from Argentina to Colombia. Colombia's [Ley 51 holiday framework](https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma_pdf.php?i=4954)
and the official [Carnival-specific administrative explanation](https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma_pdf.php?i=191351)
do not establish Carnival as a nationwide holiday. Local closures require their
own dated authority. These references were located through official search
excerpts; full-page retrieval failed during this review. Recheck the complete
texts before operational calendar use.

Barranquilla is mentioned for context; its Carnival is a different event and
location. Its programme does not establish a Medellín Carnival season.
