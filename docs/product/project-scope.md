# Consolidated Project Scope

JFXAI4MAD is a proposed modular platform combining destination travel, cross-border marriage-service coordination, professional collaboration and voluntary adult social discovery. The project also maintains a separate institutional track for FOSS simulation education, international collaboration and technology-hub development.

The repository currently contains documentation, concept images and requirements diagrams. Services, integrations, commercial partnerships and funding pathways remain proposals unless separately documented as implemented.

## Core domains

| Domain | Purpose | Operational boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Destination tourism and culture | Destinations, hotels, cruises, itineraries, events and concierge services | A booking never automatically enrolls a traveler in social discovery |
| Marriage services and LegalTech | Civil-marriage coordination, destination weddings and case tracking | Each adult consents separately; legal status and symbolic celebrations remain distinct |
| Community and social discovery | Interest-based networking, events and introductions | Adult-only, reciprocal opt-in and withdrawal controls |
| Professional collaboration and investment | B2B partnerships, ventures and institution-led projects | Professional profiles do not expose intimate preferences or legal-case records |
| Private support and family events | Confidential assistance, accessibility and family-event logistics | Minors may participate only in age-appropriate family contexts and never in adult discovery |

## Product positioning

The commercial platform is designed around delivered services rather than monetization of intimate data or vulnerability. The architecture separates professional, travel, legal and adult social contexts to reduce cross-domain inference and accidental disclosure.

## Jurisdiction-sensitive services

Remote marriage and related civil procedures are jurisdiction-specific. The platform should use official sources and qualified review for each supported workflow and must not promise universal recognition, fixed certificate timelines, automatic immigration benefits or automatic dissolution.

## Repository implementation status

The project should clearly distinguish among:

- **implemented software**;
- **reference architecture**;
- **requirements and design artifacts**;
- **market or policy research**; and
- **future roadmap items**.

This distinction should remain visible in future README and release documentation.
