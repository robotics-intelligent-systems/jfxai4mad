# Product Requirements and Consent Model

## Evidence-to-requirement rule

Exploratory market notes are hypotheses, not representative statistical evidence. Product behavior should rely on declared user intent and verified operational data rather than inferred attraction, wealth, relationship availability or suitability.

| Analysis topic | Evidence assessment | Product requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Occupation and financial attractiveness | Narrative claims do not establish reliable attraction probabilities | Use optional interest/lifestyle tags; never rank people by job title or presumed income |
| Relationship expectations | Preferences vary by person and jurisdiction | Store explicit, revocable preferences and keep them separate from legal marriage forms |
| Shift patterns and schedules | Work schedules may affect availability | Use user-selected contact windows and travel dates |
| University and business communities | Membership needs appropriate verification | Offer opt-in communities with limited disclosure and credential checks where justified |

## Consent requirements

The system should record separate approval for:

- travel planning;
- legal-service coordination;
- accommodation sharing;
- professional collaboration;
- social introductions;
- sharing selected data with a provider; and
- any optional follow-up communication.

Consent must be specific, revocable and auditable.

## Sensitive inference restrictions

The platform must not infer intimate preferences, sexual behavior, relationship status, fertility intentions or vulnerability from professional profiles, demographic proxies, location history, occupation, nationality, income or social-media language.

## Human review

Sensitive legal, identity and safety decisions require accountable human review. AI may organize evidence or draft explanations but should not make final determinations about legal capacity, document validity, civil status or eligibility.
