## 1. Executive Summary

This document establishes the strategic, legal, and demographic framework for integrating an Open-Source Software (FOSS) simulation ecosystem—including CAD/MBD (Model-Based Design) components such as the **Stavatti Stiletto** concept—into pre-military naval aviation academies. 

To counter severe demographic declines, rural depopulation, and brain drain, the project pairs technical training with **Voluntary Service Agreements (VSAs)** linked to state-backed housing and family incentives across high-urgency cities in North America, Europe, and Asia.

## 2. City-Specific Demographic & Incentive Breakdown

### A. United States
* **Tulsa, Oklahoma (*Tulsa Remote*):** Offers a direct **$10,000 USD grant** toward home purchase or cash stipends, alongside free co-working space memberships. Targets tech workers and AI/FOSS developers to revitalize regional innovation hubs (*Tulsa Innovation Labs*).
* **Marmarth, North Dakota & Topeka, Kansas (*Choose Topeka*):** Provide up to **$10,000–$15,000 USD** in relocation incentives for professionals purchasing homes or accepting local tech/engineering employment.

### B. Spain
* **Ponga (Asturias):** Grants **€3,000** for relocation plus **€3,000 for every child born** in the municipality to counter severe aging.
* **Rubiá (Galicia):** Provides monthly housing stipends of **€100–€150** to supplement living costs for new residents and remote tech workers.
* **Griegos (Teruel / "España Vaciada"):** Offers **3 months of free rent**, followed by subsidized rent (€225/month), plus **€50 per school-aged child** to preserve local educational and technical infrastructure.

### C. Germany
* **Altena (North Rhine-Westphalia):** Known for its aggressive municipal repopulation strategy, offering subsidized housing, integration support, and municipal grants for young families and skilled workers.
* **Görlitz (Saxony / *Görlitz Testing*):** Features "probationary living" (*Probewohnen*) programs offering **1 month of free housing and workspace** for remote tech professionals, open-source developers, and digital creators testing relocation.

### D. South Korea
* **Chuncheon (Gangwon-do):** Part of the Gangwon AI Cluster, offering direct municipal housing subsidies, regional tech-settlement allowances, and monthly child-rearing grants (*Parent Allowance* up to $750/month) to combat the region's TFR of **0.72**.
* **Jeonju (Jeollabuk-do):** Implements local youth housing interest-rate subsidies and startup incubation funding for open-source and smart-city software engineers.

### E. Japan
* **Kamiyama (Tokushima Prefecture):** A pioneer in rural satellite offices, transforming traditional *Akiya* (vacant homes) into high-speed fiber-connected workspaces for FOSS and AI developers.
* **Higashikawa (Hokkaido):** Offers subsidized municipal housing loans and family settlement grants for young technical professionals relocating to rural areas.

### F. Italy & Romania
* **Santo Stefano di Sessanio (Abruzzo, Italy):** Grants up to **€20,000** for new business/tech ventures plus nominal-rent housing for young residents under 40.
* **Cluj-Napoca (Transylvania, Romania):** Applies a **0% income tax exemption** for IT and software developers, paired with low operational costs for open-source simulation research.
