# Framework Collaboration Agreement

## Inter-University Volunteering for Rural Highland Development

> **Status:** conceptual proposal. Participation, funding, academic recognition and institutional approval must be confirmed directly with each university before being presented as current commitments.

## 1. Overview and rationale

The proposal outlines a strategic alliance among:

- **National University of Colombia (UNAL)**.
- **National University of Cuyo (UNCUYO, Argentina)**.
- **University of Granada (UGR, Spain)**.

The objective is to organize a transdisciplinary university-volunteering program focused on sustainable development, food sovereignty and socioeconomic strengthening of mountain and highland communities.

## 2. Proposed stakeholder and role map

| University | Proposed role | Described strengths | Proposed engagement mechanism |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UNAL | Host / territorial coordinator | Rural presence, community work and Andean watershed management | Solidarity extension and academic placements |
| UNCUYO | Methodological and technical partner | Technologies for arid/high-mountain areas and territorial approaches | Social Educational Practices and extension projects |
| UGR | Innovation and co-funding partner | Mountain agroecology and water management | Development cooperation and applicable international programs |

## 3. Intervention areas

### 3.1 Food sovereignty and agroecology

- Optimization of high-altitude crops.
- Recovery of native seeds.
- Sustainable management of hillside soils.

### 3.2 Efficient water management

- Recovery and maintenance of local irrigation infrastructure.
- Micro-watershed management.

### 3.3 Community health and rural habitat

- Primary-health prevention.
- Water treatment.
- Sustainable habitability.

### 3.4 Local socioeconomic development

- Strengthening farmer associations.
- Community-based agritourism.
- Rural digitalization.

## 4. Proposed volunteer profile

- Advanced undergraduate or graduate students.
- Fields: Agronomy, Social Sciences, Environmental Engineering, Health Sciences and Geography.
- Willingness to work with communities and under high-mountain conditions.

## 5. Proposed academic recognition

| Institution | Proposed mechanism |
| --- | --- |
| UNAL | Solidarity-extension credits or social internship, subject to approval |
| UNCUYO | Hours within social educational practices, subject to current regulations |
| UGR | ECTS credits or cooperation/volunteering recognition, subject to approval |
