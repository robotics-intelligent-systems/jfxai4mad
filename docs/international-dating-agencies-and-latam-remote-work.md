# International Dating Agencies and the LATAM Remote-Work Market

> **Editorial status:** the original file combined two independent topics. This version separates them to avoid inappropriate associations. Demographic and market figures should be verified before reuse.

## 1. International dating and marriage agencies

The original document describes the historical presence of Eastern European countries—including Ukraine, Russia, Belarus, Moldova, Romania and Poland—in international dating and marriage services.

It also mentions the U.S. **International Marriage Broker Regulation Act (IMBRA)** as a protective framework related to certain international intermediaries.

### Interpretation limits

- Nationality does not establish interest in marriage, migration or any particular relationship.
- Average ages of agency profiles do not represent the general population.
- Any current analysis should use recent statistics and identifiable sources.
- Consent, safety, fraud prevention and individual autonomy should be prioritized.

## 2. Remote-work market in Latin America

### Countries highlighted in the original document

- **Brazil and Argentina:** software, data science and engineering.

## 3. Documentation-architecture recommendation

Remote-employment analysis should remain an **independent professional work package**, separate from dating services, personal affinity or migration decisions. Employment, investment, personal relationships and legal residence require distinct processes and consents.
