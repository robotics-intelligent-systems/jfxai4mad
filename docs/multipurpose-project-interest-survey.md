## 2. Participation model

- [ ] **100% pro bono / volunteering:** contribution without expected financial return.
- [ ] **Commercial / results-based commission:** paid participation based on sales or resources generated, with written terms.
- [ ] **Hybrid model:** limited volunteer tasks plus a clearly defined results-based commission.
- [ ] **Founding partner / co-investor:** capital or equipment contribution under a separate investment agreement.

> Paid, volunteer and investment status should be clearly distinguished before activities begin.

## 3. Contribution area

- [ ] Commercial and sales.
- [ ] Technical development / engineering / IT.
- [ ] Administrative, legal or financial management.
- [ ] Operations, logistics and customer service.

## 4. Primary motivation

- [ ] Generate additional income through a transparent model.
- [ ] Gain practical experience and portfolio material.
- [ ] Generate positive social impact.
- [ ] Connect with a collaborative professional network.

## 5. Initial availability

- [ ] **Occasional:** a few hours per week or specific tasks.
- [ ] **Part-time:** approximately 5–10 hours per week.
- [ ] **Project-based:** participation during a defined scope and period.
