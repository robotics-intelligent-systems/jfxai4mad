# Curriculum: AI-Assisted Sales, Cloud and Natural Language

**Proposed duration:** 12 weeks  
**Proposed instructional load:** 90 hours + practical projects  
**Format:** theory and practice

## 1. Objective

Train professionals to combine consultative sales, cloud tools, CRM, generative AI and multilingual communication in B2B/B2C environments.

## 2. Modular structure

### Module 1 — Modern sales and cloud fundamentals

#### Week 1 — Consultative sales and SaaS

- SPIN Selling.
- BANT.
- Challenger Sale.
- Basic AWS, Google Cloud and Azure concepts oriented to business value.

#### Week 2 — Cloud tools for commercial management

- Cloud CRM.
- Prospect tracking.
- Basic workflow automation.

### Module 2 — AI and natural-language processing

#### Week 3 — Generative AI and commercial prompts

- Account research.
- Email personalization.
- Segmentation.
- Prompt engineering applied to sales.

#### Week 4 — Virtual assistants and natural language

- Conversational agents for lead qualification.
- Sentiment analysis as support, not a substitute for professional judgment.

### Module 3 — Multilingual strategy and global prospecting

#### Week 5 — AI-assisted multilingual sales

- Assisted translation and interpretation.
- Cultural adaptation of commercial messaging.

#### Week 6 — Multichannel prospecting and market intelligence

- LinkedIn, email and messaging, respecting terms of service and anti-spam law.
- Competitive research and public sources.
- Data collection only through lawful and ethical means.

### Module 4 — Argumentation, objections and closing

#### Week 7 — Call and demonstration simulation

- Practice bots or assistants.
- Personalized presentations and proposals.

#### Week 8 — Sales analytics

- CAC.
- LTV.
- Conversion rate.
- Pipeline velocity.
- Conversation analytics with necessary authorizations.

### Module 5 — Integrating project and professional brand

#### Weeks 9–11

Design a multilingual campaign integrating CRM, automation and a conversational agent.

#### Week 12

- Professional-profile optimization.
- Interview preparation.
- Presentation of the integrating project.

## 3. Graduate profile

Graduates should be able to:

- use CRM and cloud tools;
- apply generative AI to commercial tasks;
- adapt messages across markets and languages;
- interpret commercial metrics;
- automate repetitive tasks while retaining human oversight.

## 4. Ethical and legal considerations

- Comply with platform terms of service.
- Respect privacy, consent and anti-spam laws.
- Do not use scraping or data enrichment in ways that violate technical or legal restrictions.
- Maintain human review for high-impact decisions.
