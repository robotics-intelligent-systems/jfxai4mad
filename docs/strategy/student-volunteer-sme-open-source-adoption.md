# Student Volunteer and SME Open-Source Adoption Strategy

**Status:** proposed programme and illustrative recruitment dialogues. Benefits, free-service scope and future support offers describe the source proposal; they are not evidence of an operating programme or guaranteed outcomes.

## Three-way benefit model

The strategy proposes a win-win-win model:

- **Small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs):** technological modernization with no charge for software or basic implementation hours.
- **Students:** real technical experience, a professional portfolio and mentoring.
- **Organization / open-source project:** market reach, software adoption and positioning for future advanced support and consulting services.

## Conversation simulations

### Dialogue 1: Recruiting and onboarding student volunteers

**Objective:** persuade a student to contribute time to software migration/configuration in exchange for practical experience and certificates documenting real impact.

**Channels:** an academic event, LinkedIn, or an in-person/virtual meeting.

**Recruiter / coordinator:** Hi, Mateo! I noticed you are in the final semesters of Systems Engineering / Software Development. How is your professional portfolio coming along as you prepare to enter the job market?

**Student (Mateo):** Hi. I am working on it. I have completed several academic projects and GitHub exercises, but employers ask for experience in real environments, with actual clients and projects deployed to production.

**Recruiter:** That is exactly why I am reaching out. We have launched a community initiative for practical experience with free/open-source software. We connect students with real SMEs that need to digitize their processes. You handle implementation, configuration and deployment of open-source ERP/CRM software with support from experienced mentors.

**Student:** That sounds interesting, but what do I gain beyond practice if the service is voluntary?

**Recruiter:** You gain three key things:

1. **A verifiable portfolio:** real success stories you can show future employers.
2. **A recommendation letter and certificate of hours:** issued by the organization and validated by the participating SME.
3. **Professional contacts and mentoring:** you learn good practices in architecture, server deployment and customer service alongside senior professionals.

**Student:** I am interested. What is the time commitment?

**Recruiter:** It is flexible: between **5 and 8 hours per week**, over a **4-to-6-week cycle per project**. We assign you a mentor and an SME suited to your current skills. Would you join the next participant cohort?

**Student:** Absolutely! What is the next step to register?

### Dialogue 2: SME recruitment and negotiation — migration case

**Objective:** overcome the SME owner's distrust, reduce reservations about “free/open-source” software and obtain acceptance of the service.

**Channels:** a sales visit, cold call or assessment session.

**Business consultant:** Good morning, Mr. Carlos. I noticed that your distribution business currently manages inventory and invoicing with Excel spreadsheets and notebooks. How much time do you spend reconciling those records each day?

**Client (Mr. Carlos — SME):** A great deal. Sometimes records also get misplaced. I have wanted to buy a system, but software licences are very expensive, with high monthly charges per user.

**Business consultant:** I understand. We run a Free/Open-Source Software Adoption Programme. We offer to migrate your management processes to an open-source ERP system: no lifetime licence payments, multiuser access and adaptation to your business.

**Client:** No licence payments? What is the catch? Nobody gives away software and work for no reason.

**Business consultant:** Let me explain transparently:

- **The software:** it is open source, developed globally and available for free, lawful use.
- **The implementation team:** an interdisciplinary group of advanced technology students works under direct supervision from senior mentors.
- **The exchange:** your company receives installation, data migration and training at no charge. In return, you provide a real environment where students gain experience and, at completion, a testimonial or acceptance letter.

**Client:** I am concerned that students might make mistakes with my business data.

**Business consultant:** That is a valid concern. The process follows three safety rules:

1. **Isolation:** we first work on a test server with backup copies.
2. **Validation:** no change reaches your final system without review by the responsible senior mentor.
3. **Training:** we teach your staff to use the tool before retiring the Excel spreadsheets.

**Client:** I understand. What if I later need maintenance or help with technical failures?

**Business consultant:** Basic support is covered during the programme. At completion, your team is trained to operate the system independently. If your company later grows and needs complex integrations or dedicated servers, we offer professional maintenance plans, while you retain your working software without lock-in.

**Client:** That sounds reasonable. What do we need to start?

**Business consultant:** Let us schedule a **30-minute session** this week to review your current data sheets and structure the migration plan. Would Thursday morning work for you?

## Key success factors

| Pillar | Key action | Strategic benefit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quality control | Assign one senior professional / mentor to each group of **2–3 students**. | Prevent operational errors at the SME and protect the project's reputation. |
| Confidentiality policy | Sign nondisclosure agreements (NDAs) between the SME and volunteers. | Reassure the company about handling financial and commercial data. |
| Clear deliverables | Define scope to avoid endless requests for custom development. | Keep projects within the estimated **4–6 weeks**. |
| Future monetization model | Offer managed services: service-level agreements (SLAs), managed hosting and cloud backups. | Convert satisfied SMEs into recurring paying customers over the medium term. |
