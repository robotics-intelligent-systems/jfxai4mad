# Recruiter Partnership Outreach Template

[Documentation index](../README.md) · [Strategy index](README.md)

## Purpose

A reusable English message for exploring a professional recruiting partnership.
Replace placeholders with verified facts before use. The original draft mixed a
generic company placeholder with a named company; this version consistently uses
`[Company]` and does not assert unverified vacancies or an established talent network.

## Message template

**Subject:** Senior talent collaboration / LATAM recruiting partnership

Hello [Recruiter name],

I saw your role at [Company] and your work supporting [verified client market or
service area]. I am reaching out about [verified open role or hiring priority].

My work focuses on [accurate description of your engineering experience or
professional collaboration activity]. I would like to explore whether I could
support your team as a complementary channel for senior talent or technical
collaboration across [regions where you actually have relevant experience or contacts].

Potential areas include [relevant, verified skills, such as React, Vue, Angular,
Java or .NET]. If there is a fit, we could discuss a results-based collaboration,
co-development or referral arrangement with clear responsibilities and terms.

Would you be available for a brief 15-minute conversation this week? You can suggest
a suitable time or use [scheduling link, if available].

Best regards,

[Name]
[Professional profile or portfolio link]

## Adaptation notes

- Mention U.S./European client markets or Latin American/Eastern European networks
  only when those details are accurate and relevant to the recipient.
- Verify the role and skills instead of listing every technology in the source.
- Do not promise accelerated placement, available candidates or commission terms
  that have not been agreed.
- A Google Calendar/Meet link may be used when an appropriate scheduling workflow
  exists; remove the placeholder otherwise.
- Share candidate information only with their permission. This is a message draft,
  not an instruction to contact anyone automatically.
