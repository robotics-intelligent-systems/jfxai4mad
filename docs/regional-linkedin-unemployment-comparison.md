## Methodological considerations

1. A LinkedIn registered-member count is not equivalent to active users.
2. Unemployment rates should be compared using the same reference period and compatible labor definitions.
3. Latin America is a multinational region, while California, Florida and Hawaii are U.S. states and Puerto Rico is a U.S. territory; the geographic levels are therefore not equivalent.
4. Aggregate unemployment must not be used to infer availability, economic need or professional interest of a specific person.
