# Project: Collaborative Connection Platform and Compatibility Algorithms

> **Editorial status:** exploratory proposal. Dating platforms and professional platforms should remain separate according to their purposes and terms of service.

## 1. Executive summary

The proposal studies whether compatibility systems can help identify adults with shared interests in social-impact projects, volunteering, sustainability or consensual forms of cohabitation.

## 2. Objectives

### General objective

Evaluate affinity mechanisms that can facilitate voluntary connections among adults with compatible interests.

### Specific objectives

- Design values-and-interests questionnaires.
- Explore clear-communication and relationship-responsibility dynamics.
- Compare aggregate patterns across regions without inferring sensitive individual attributes.
- Separate the relationship component from the professional component.

## 3. Conceptual methodology

```text
Phase 1 — Criteria configuration
├─ Values and interests
├─ Voluntary questions
└─ Define the purpose of each channel

Phase 2 — Affinity discovery
├─ Initial conversation
├─ Verify expectations
└─ Consent to continue

Phase 3 — Separate collaboration, when appropriate
├─ Move to an appropriate professional channel
├─ Define scope and terms
└─ Independent agreement
```

## 4. Application areas

- Rural and community development.
- Skills-based volunteering.
- Co-housing or other adult living arrangements when there is independent, explicit interest.

## 5. Proposed indicators

- Declared compatibility in voluntary questions.
- Continued-conversation rate.
- Number of professional collaborations started through a separate process.
- Satisfaction and clarity of consent.

## 6. Platform and privacy boundaries

A `match` or compatibility percentage should not be used as:

- an employability assessment;
- an indicator of financial capacity;
- a prediction of willingness to marry, cohabit or engage in intimacy;
- authorization for commercial messages.

When mutual professional interest emerges, collaboration should move to an explicit, separate professional process with transparent terms.
