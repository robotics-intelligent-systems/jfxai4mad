# Inbound International Tourism Comparison: Peru and Colombia

> **Editorial status:** migrated and reformatted from the original plaintext file. The figures are historical approximations and should be checked against current official statistics.

## 1. Peru

The original document places annual inbound volume at approximately **3.2–3.5 million** international visitors.

| Rank | Origin market | Approximate share | Described characteristics |
| ---: | --- | ---: | --- |
| 1 | Chile | ~28–32% | Tacna–Arica border traffic, tourism and business |
| 2 | United States | ~14–16% | Cultural and archaeological tourism: Machu Picchu, Sacred Valley and Inca Trail |
| 3 | Ecuador | ~8–10% | Cross-border flows, commerce and northern beaches |
| 4 | Colombia | ~5–7% | Leisure tourism, gastronomy and business |
| 5 | Brazil | ~4–5% | Cultural, gastronomic and higher-end tourism |
| 6 | Europe (Spain, France, Germany) | ~13–15% | Longer stays and southern circuit/Amazon travel |

## 2. Colombia

The original document places annual inbound volume at approximately **6.5–6.9 million** international visitors.

| Rank | Origin market | Approximate share | Described characteristics |
| ---: | --- | ---: | --- |
| 1 | United States | ~25–27% | Cartagena, Medellín, Bogotá, gastronomy and diaspora travel |
| 2 | Mexico | ~6–8% | Air connectivity to Bogotá, Medellín and the Caribbean |
| 3 | Ecuador | ~5–7% | Border integration and leisure tourism |
| 4 | Venezuela | ~5–6% | Border, family and commercial flows |
| 5 | Peru | ~4–5% | Colombian Caribbean, Bogotá and meetings tourism |
| 6 | Europe (Spain, France, Germany) | ~10–12% | Nature, Coffee Region and historic cities |

## 3. Summary comparison

- **Peru:** the document assigns greater relative weight to neighboring markets, especially Chile, and to cultural, historical and gastronomic tourism.
- **Colombia:** the document assigns greater individual weight to the United States and a more diversified offer spanning urban tourism, meetings, biodiversity and sun/beach travel.
- **Volume:** the original text considers Colombia's international volume to be close to twice Peru's.

## 4. Sources requiring verification

Before publishing updated figures, consult official statistics from **MINCETUR/Migraciones Perú** and **MINCIT/ProColombia**, identifying the year, visitor definition and methodology.
