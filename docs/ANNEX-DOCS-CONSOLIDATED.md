## 1. Documentation migration and normalization

The documentation migration applies the following rules:

- English filenames and English prose;
- Markdown heading hierarchy, tables and lists;
- Mermaid diagrams where they improve process clarity;
- relative links to repository-local documents and image assets;
- editorial notes that distinguish inherited claims from verified facts;
- separation of professional, legal, medical, adult-relationship, safeguarding and public-policy topics;
- explicit distinction between consensual adult sex work and trafficking, coercion, exploitation or sexual violence;
- no operational guidance for evading platform moderation or targeting individuals for sexual or compensated arrangements.

The JPG assets remain in image format and retain their standardized English filenames.

[`student-sex-work-and-sugar-dating-regional-analysis.md`](./student-sex-work-and-sugar-dating-regional-analysis.md)

### 7.4 University sex-work estimates and platform integrity

[`university-sex-work-estimates-and-platform-integrity.md`](./university-sex-work-estimates-and-platform-integrity.md)

### 7.5 Editorial safeguards for this section

Across these four documents:

- consensual adult sex work is not conflated with trafficking or coercion;
- no individual should be profiled as a sex worker or trafficking victim based on age, university, occupation, income, nationality, ethnicity, photos or online-profile language;
- prevalence estimates require identifiable source populations, dates and definitions;
- professional platforms should not be used to conceal prohibited sexual-service solicitation;
- student support should prioritize financial aid, housing, digital safety, health, violence prevention and confidential referral pathways.
